About Cooper

Prototype for NASA Space Apps 2026 · Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data

Cooper helps farmers think; it doesn't decide for them. Every result comes from written agronomic rules and published formulas that anyone can check. The only AI is optional: it rewrites the narration of “Explain it simply” and answers questions in Jarvis using the numbers of your analysis.

Data: NASA POWER (power.larc.nasa.gov) · NASA SRTM via OpenTopoData · NASA GIBS (MODIS NDVI) · ISRIC SoilGrids · ESA WorldCover · Open-Meteo · Imagery © Esri · Map © OpenStreetMap. It does not replace the judgment of farmers or agronomists.
